# AfriSphere AI — Market Research

## Africa tourism market (2025 data)

- **81.3 million** international arrivals to Africa in 2025 — the fastest-growing tourism region in the world, up **8% year-on-year**.
  Source: UN Tourism / UNWTO

- Africa's travel market is valued at **$25.73 billion** in 2025, projected to reach **$39.92 billion by 2034** (5% CAGR).
  Source: Market Data Forecast

## Zimbabwe tourism market (2025 data)

- Zimbabwe's international arrivals grew approximately **10%** to **1.78 million** in 2025.
  Source: ZimStat

- In Q3 2025, Zimbabwe recorded **520,751 foreign visitors**, up **15% year-on-year**, with **58%** arriving from within Africa.
  Source: ZimStat

## What this means for AfriSphere AI

- Africa is a large and accelerating tourism market, not a niche one.
- A majority of Zimbabwe's visitors are regional (African) travellers — a segment global AI travel tools are rarely built for, and one where local language/context (Shona, Ndebele) is a genuine advantage.
- Zimbabwe's growth rate outpaces the already-fast continental average, supporting the "Zimbabwe first" launch strategy as a real opportunity, not just a convenient starting point.
