## Hotels

### Ilala Lodge Hotel — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
A luxury, family-run hotel in the centre of Victoria Falls, directly
bordering Victoria Falls National Park and about an 8-minute walk from the
Falls entrance. 73 rooms across Classic Rooms, Classic Suites, Deluxe
Rooms, Executive Suites, and The Strathearn Suite. On-site Cassia
Restaurant, two pools, a spa, conference facilities, and a Tours &
Activities Desk. Wildlife (warthogs, occasionally elephants) is sometimes
visible on the grounds, since the property borders the national park.
Published rates (subject to change): Classic Room ~$246/night sharing,
Deluxe ~$284/night, Classic Suite ~$351/night.

### Somalisa Camp — Hwange National Park, Zimbabwe
A luxury tented safari camp with 7 tented suites, known for close-up
elephant encounters at the camp's Elephant Pool. Strong wildlife area for
lion, leopard, cheetah, African wild dog, and antelope species. Activities
include game drives, walking safaris, birding, and community visits.
About a 45-minute flight from Victoria Falls. Solar-powered with a
water-recycling system.

### The Hide Safari Camp — Hwange National Park, Zimbabwe
A long-established luxury safari camp on a private concession on the
eastern boundary of Hwange, with 10 deluxe tents. Overlooks a
wildlife-rich waterhole, with an underground hide for direct wildlife
viewing. Particularly strong for elephant and buffalo, with chances of
lion, leopard, and African wild dog. Features Dove's Nest, a private
treehouse sleep-out. About 2.5–3 hours by road from Victoria Falls
Airport, or ~35 minutes by light aircraft.

### The Victoria Falls Hotel — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
One of Zimbabwe's most historic luxury hotels, built in 1904 with
Edwardian character. Five-star, 149 rooms and suites. Views toward the
Victoria Falls gorge and Victoria Falls Bridge from parts of the property.
Multiple dining experiences with internationally trained chefs. Best for
travellers wanting historic luxury and easy access to the Falls rather
than a remote safari experience.

### Victoria Falls Safari Lodge — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
A luxury safari-style lodge just outside Victoria Falls town, 72
sunset-facing rooms, set on a plateau overlooking a wildlife waterhole in
Zambezi National Park. Elephants, buffalo, and kudu are regular visitors
to the waterhole. Hosts The Boma – Dinner & Drum Show on site. Restaurant,
Buffalo Bar, swimming pool, and complimentary shuttle to the Victoria
Falls rainforest and town.

## Restaurants

### The Boma – Dinner & Drum Show — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
A Zimbabwean cultural dining experience combining traditional food, music,
and live drumming. Authentic Zimbabwean cuisine.

### Victoria Falls Eatery — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
A relaxed local dining option for travellers exploring Victoria Falls.
Authentic Zimbabwean cuisine.

### Homely House Restaurant — Bulawayo, Zimbabwe
A local-style dining experience focused on traditional Zimbabwean cuisine
and familiar local flavours. Authentic Zimbabwean cuisine.

### The Lookout Cafe — Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
A scenic Victoria Falls dining option with views over the surrounding
landscape. International/African fusion, not specifically traditional
Zimbabwean.
